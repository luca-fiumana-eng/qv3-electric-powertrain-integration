# FEM Study Summary — Aluminium Rear Swing Arm

## Source
- Report: `EQV3 Rear Swing Arm Al Alloy FEM analysis`
- Number/revision: `tec.rep.873`, revision `00`
- Date: `20/11/2019`
- Software visible in screenshots: ANSYS R17.2

The report's objective is to verify the aluminium rear swing-arm version on the EQV3 vehicle. It refers to report `rep.771` for further FEM model and constraint details.

## Material assumption
Generic aluminium alloy; assumed yield strength `140 MPa`. No alloy designation or temper is specified.

## Load case 1 — vertical
- 2.5 g vertical acceleration.
- Fully loaded vehicle.
- Belt traction: 3,386 N.
- Maximum stress legend value visible: approximately 455.73 MPa.

## Load case 2 — braking
- 1.0 g braking deceleration and 1.0 g vertical acceleration.
- Fully loaded vehicle.
- Belt traction: 3,386 N.
- The report assumes all braking force is generated by the rear disc.
- Maximum stress legend value visible: approximately 990.88 MPa.

Both displayed local maxima exceed the assumed 140 MPa yield strength. They should be treated as critical findings requiring engineering investigation, not as acceptable stress results.

## Torsional stiffness
The report applies a sample torque of 100 Nm and compares:
- Previous steel version (80 × 45 central tube): 690 Nm/°.
- New aluminium-alloy version: 826 Nm/°.

Relative increase: `(826 - 690) / 690 × 100 = 19.7%`.

This comparison is limited to the model setup and assumptions in the source report; no physical test correlation is stated.

## Conclusion in the source report
The report explicitly states: **“The link from caliper support and swing arm have to be modified.”**

## Information not available
Exact aluminium grade/temper, mesh statistics and convergence, element formulation, complete contact and bolt assumptions, full displacement results, fatigue analysis, physical correlation and updated results after the proposed connection modification are not included in the supplied report.
